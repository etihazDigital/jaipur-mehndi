About the Studio

Rooted in Rajasthan, Refined for Every Bride

Since 2014, Jaipur Mehandi Special has been crafting bridal and celebration mehandi from our studio in Vidyadhar Nagar, Jaipur. What began as a passion for the fine, storytelling patterns of traditional Rajasthani mehandi has grown into a dedicated bridal art practice — trusted for detailed full-arm bridal designs, portrait bridal mehandi and elegant personalised motifs.

Every design is planned around you — your outfit, your jewellery and the story you want your hands to tell on your big day.

What We Offer

Mehandi Artistry, Tailored to the Occasion

From elaborate bridal storytelling to delicate personal keepsakes, every design is hand-drawn to suit the moment.

Signature Bridal Mehandi

Elaborate full-arm and full-leg bridal designs featuring fine motifs, palace patterns and portrait artistry for the bride who wants it all.

Party & Guest Mehandi

Graceful hand and palm designs for sangeet, mehandi ceremonies and family functions — quick, elegant and photo-ready.

Mini & Personalised Mehandi

Names, initials, dates and meaningful symbols worked into delicate keepsake designs — perfect for engagements and anniversaries.

Arabic & Minimal Designs

Flowing floral trails and clean, minimal patterns for those who love mehandi that is understated yet striking.
